Kloud Knit Mill Ltd, located in Tirupur, is a manufacturer of knitted garments. We specialize in utilizing the unutilized capacity within the knit garment manufacturing value chain, thereby offering our B2B customers a unique and seamless manufacturing experience.

Our approach involves aggregating, integrating, facilitating, and connecting the right suppliers in the knit garment value chain to our B2B customers. By doing so, we create value for both our knit suppliers and customers, ensuring a mutually beneficial relationship.

In Tirupur, there is a significant amount of unutilized capacity across various stages of the knit garment manufacturing process, including Spinning, Knitting, Processing, and Cut-to-Pack units. These inefficiencies arise from issues such as waiting for materials, unnecessary changeovers, lower production efficiency, and limited orders. At Kloud Knit Mill, we address these challenges by introducing value chain transparency and streamlining operations.

We work closely with our suppliers, aligning their strengths with the specific product requirements of our customers. This strategic approach minimizes downtime and maximizes productivity, resulting in optimized production efficiency. Additionally, our technical assistance and support enable our suppliers to overcome challenges and enhance their manufacturing capabilities.

At Kloud Knit Mill, we leverage machine data analytics, including descriptive, predictive, and prescriptive analytics, to gain valuable insights into production processes. This data-driven approach helps us identify bottlenecks, forecast demand, and make informed decisions, ensuring efficient operations and timely delivery.

Our commitment is to deliver the right quality garments to our customers at transparent and competitive prices. We understand the importance of timely delivery in the fast-paced fashion industry, and our streamlined processes and optimized supply chain enable us to meet shorter lead times.

Join us at Kloud Knit Mill and experience the benefits of our integrated and customer-centric approach. Together, we can deliver high-quality knitted garments with transparency, efficiency, and competitive pricing, ensuring customer satisfaction and long-term partnerships.
